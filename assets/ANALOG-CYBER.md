# Studio 84 faceplate

`analog-cyber-faceplate.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-21 for the full 1980s rack redesign. It replaces the earlier nickel/brass material and is embedded in the plugin binary. All controls, labels, seven-segment readouts, response plots, and meters render live.

`analog-cyber-preview.png` is a native JUCE editor capture with audio driving the meters.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: background-texture. Asset type: actual raster faceplate material for the openMaster VST audio plugin, live controls are drawn separately. Generate a refined 1980s professional studio rack-unit black anodized aluminum faceplate. Flat orthographic continuous rectangular surface, 2:1 landscape aspect ratio, fills image edge to edge. Almost-black neutral charcoal, very fine horizontal brushed machining grain and subtle satin reflections; the central 90% stays uniformly dark and calm. Slightly darker edges with a restrained black beveled rim at the outermost 1%. Premium understated vintage digital audio hardware, perfectly clean, softly lit. No gold, no bronze, no bright metallic trim, no colored lighting, no bright perimeter, no neon, no gradients of color, no perspective. No text, no labels, no graphics, no logos, no screws, no knobs, no meters, no divisions or controls. This is only the blank charcoal metal material used behind functional controls, not a finished UI mockup.
